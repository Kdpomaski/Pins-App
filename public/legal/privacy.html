Pins Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

Pins is published by 220 TECH LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy describes how the Pins mobile and web apps handle information.

What Pins stores on your device

Injection logs, inventory, schedules, and optional lock settings stay on your device. They are encrypted locally (AES-256-GCM). We cannot read this data from our servers because it is not uploaded by default.

Account information

If you create an account, we store your email through our authentication provider (Supabase) so you can sign in. During onboarding we ask for age range and gender for anonymous, aggregate product stats only. We do not sell this information.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers for ads
  • No analytics SDKs
  • No sale of personal data
  • Health or protocol data is not sent to us unless you later enable an explicit cloud backup feature

Third parties

Sign-in may use Supabase and, if you choose Google, Google sign-in. Those providers process your email under their own policies.

Your choices

You can sign out, delete local app data by uninstalling, or email us to delete your account. Optional device lock (passphrase) never leaves the device.

Children

Pins is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account for a child under 13.

Medical disclaimer

Pins is a personal organization tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Always consult a qualified clinician.
